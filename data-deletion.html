You can ask us to review a request relating to personal information held by Contribution Management Bot. Requests may relate to access, correction, objection, restriction or deletion, subject to applicable law and the rights of other people.

How to submit a request

  1. Email johnhappinessstanley@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line Privacy or Data Deletion Request – Contribution Management Bot.
  3. State whether the request concerns a user account, WhatsApp number, group membership, member record, occasion record or financial record.
  4. Provide the group name (if applicable) and the WhatsApp telephone number or account email associated with the record. Do not email passwords, one-time codes, M-PESA PINs or banking credentials.
  5. We may ask for reasonable information to verify your identity and authority to make the request.

Requests about group records

If your information was entered by a group or organisation, contact that group's Chairperson or authorised administrator as well. The group may determine why member, occasion and contribution records are held and who is authorised to manage them. We will coordinate with the appropriate group contact where necessary.

What happens next

We will review the request and respond in accordance with applicable data-protection law. We may retain certain information where required or permitted by law, where it is needed for financial or audit records, to resolve a dispute, to protect another person's rights, or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. If we cannot delete all requested information, we will explain the reason where required.

Deleting or disconnecting a WhatsApp conversation on your device does not by itself delete records already held by the Service or by Meta. A separate request may be needed for information held by Meta under its own policies.
