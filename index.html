1. Who operates the service

Contribution Management Bot (the “Service”) is a WhatsApp-based service used by groups and organisations to manage membership information, occasions, contributions, pledges, expenses and financial reports.

The Service is operated by [INSERT LEGAL NAME OF OPERATOR] (the “Operator”, “we”, “us” or “our”). For privacy questions and requests, contact johnhappinessstanley@gmail.com. This email address must be confirmed as an appropriate public privacy contact before publication.

Depending on the information involved, the group or organisation using the Service may decide why its members' information is collected and how it is used. Group administrators should be contacted first about membership, occasion and financial records held for that group. The Operator handles information needed to provide, secure and support the Service. The parties' precise data-protection roles depend on the arrangements for the particular group.

2. Information we process

The information processed depends on how a person or group uses the Service. It may include:

  • Account and registration information: name, email address, WhatsApp telephone number, account identifiers, group membership, role and account status. Verification codes are stored in hashed form and are time-limited.
  • Group and member information: group name, member number, full name, telephone number, membership status and notes supplied by an authorised group user.
  • Occasion information: occasion type and title, names of supported persons or beneficiaries, dates, venue, event details, treasurer contact information, payment instructions and announcement text. Users should avoid entering sensitive personal details unless genuinely necessary and lawfully authorised.
  • Financial and accounting information: contribution and transport amounts, pledge amounts, payment references, contribution status, expenses, fund-closing information, report details, and the identities of users who record, confirm or reverse transactions.
  • WhatsApp and conversation information: sender and recipient telephone numbers, Meta message identifiers, message type, text needed to handle a conversation, conversation state and context, outgoing message content, delivery status and technical error information. A limited inbound payload needed for processing and recovery is stored in encrypted form.
  • Spreadsheet-import information: the uploaded spreadsheet's filename and the member rows and validation results extracted to prepare an import preview. The Service accepts XLSX, XLS and CSV files within its configured size and row limits. Import previews are assigned a 24-hour expiry; expiry does not necessarily mean that every related database record is immediately erased.
  • Security and audit information: records of actions taken in the Service, relevant record references, timestamps, and (where captured) IP addresses and technical logs.

3. Why information is used

Information is used to:

  • register and verify users and manage group invitations and authorised roles;
  • maintain group membership and occasion records;
  • record received contributions, pledges, expenses and financial closings;
  • prepare contribution reports and financial statements;
  • receive and respond to WhatsApp messages and deliver requested notifications or documents;
  • prevent duplicate processing, investigate errors, recover failed operations and protect the security of the Service; and
  • meet applicable legal obligations and resolve disputes or enforce legitimate rights.

We do not use group or member data for unrelated advertising. The Service records payment amounts and references entered by authorised users; it is not itself a bank, mobile-money wallet or payment processor and does not need a user's M-PESA PIN or banking password.

4. Lawful grounds and responsibilities of groups

Personal information is processed only for specified and legitimate purposes and on a lawful basis applicable to the particular activity. Depending on the circumstances, that basis may include a person's consent, steps requested by the person, the operation of the group's agreed arrangements, a legal obligation or a legitimate interest that does not override the person's rights.

Groups and organisations must ensure they have a lawful basis and appropriate authority before uploading or entering information about their members, beneficiaries or other people. They should tell those people what information is being recorded and how it will be used. Group users should not enter another person's information merely because it is available to them.

5. Who may receive information

Information may be made available to:

  • Authorised users of the relevant group: access is controlled by assigned group roles and permissions. Group officials may see information needed for their authorised duties, including member and financial records.
  • Meta and WhatsApp: messages are sent and received through the WhatsApp Business Platform. Meta processes information under its own terms and privacy policies.
  • Infrastructure and service providers: the production hosting, database, backup, file-storage and operational providers selected for the Service may process information to operate it. The Operator must identify the actual production providers and relevant processing locations before production use.
  • Authorities or other parties: where disclosure is required by law, necessary to protect rights or safety, or required to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We do not sell personal information. Some providers may process information outside Kenya. Where personal data is transferred outside Kenya, the Operator and relevant group must ensure that applicable legal requirements and safeguards are met.

6. Security

The Service uses measures including role-based access controls, group-level access restrictions, hashed account passwords and verification codes, validation of Meta webhook signatures, and encryption of limited inbound message payloads stored for processing and recovery. Access to group records is restricted according to the user's group membership and role.

No system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect their devices and accounts, avoid sharing verification codes, passwords, M-PESA PINs or banking credentials, and report suspected unauthorised access promptly.

7. Retention

Personal information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, or for a longer period where required or permitted by law. Account, membership, financial and audit information may need to be retained to operate group records, maintain financial history, resolve disputes and meet legal or accounting obligations.

Verification codes are valid for 10 minutes. Import previews are assigned a 24-hour expiry. These application expiry settings do not, by themselves, establish a complete deletion schedule for every associated record, backup or log. The Operator must adopt and apply a category-by-category retention and deletion schedule and update this notice when that schedule is finalised.

8. Your data-protection rights

Subject to applicable law and any lawful limitations, a person may have the right to be informed about use of their personal data, request access, request correction of inaccurate or misleading information, object to processing, request erasure or restriction where applicable, and request data portability.

To exercise a right, email johnhappinessstanley@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy Request – Contribution Management Bot”. Explain the request and provide enough information for us to identify the relevant account or record. Do not send passwords, verification codes or payment PINs. We may need to verify identity before acting.

If the information belongs to a group, contact the group's Chairperson or authorised administrator as well. Some financial or audit records may need to be retained where the law permits or requires it, or where necessary to protect the rights of other people. We will explain any applicable limitation when responding.

You may also raise a concern with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya.

9. Children and sensitive information

The Service is designed for group officials and organisations managing membership and contribution records; it is not designed as a service directed to children. Group administrators must take particular care before entering information relating to a child or sensitive personal information, including information that may appear in a medical or other occasion record. Such information should only be supplied where necessary, authorised and lawfully processed.
